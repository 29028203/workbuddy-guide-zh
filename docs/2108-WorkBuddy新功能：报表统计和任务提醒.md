# WorkBuddy新功能：报表统计和任务提醒

> 原文链接：[https://www.laoshoucun.com/workbuddyzl/2108](https://www.laoshoucun.com/workbuddyzl/2108)

<p>WorkBuddy新版最实用的两个功能是：报表统计和任务提醒。<br />
<img decoding="async" loading="lazy" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2109" src="https://www.laoshoucun.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-300x199.jpeg" alt="" width="300" height="199" srcset="https://cdn.laoshoucun.com/web/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-300x199.jpeg 300w, https://cdn.laoshoucun.com/web/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-1024x680.jpeg 1024w, https://cdn.laoshoucun.com/web/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-768x510.jpeg 768w, https://cdn.laoshoucun.com/web/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-1536x1020.jpeg 1536w, https://cdn.laoshoucun.com/web/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-2048x1360.jpeg 2048w, https://cdn.laoshoucun.com/web/2026/03/fOqzfsfKH-1200x797.jpeg 1200w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 300px) 85vw, 300px" /><br />
报表统计可以自定义维度，比如任务完成率和工时统计，团队绩效。自动生成柱状图和折线图，支持导出 Excel/PDF。还能设置定时报表，自动推送邮箱，不用每次手动做。</p>
<p>任务提醒升级后支持多渠道：应用内和短信，邮件。可以提前几小时/几天提醒，逾期自动加强提醒力度。批量提醒也能一次发给所有人，非常高效。</p>
<p>这两个功能把数据统计和任务管理闭环了，适合所有团队。</p>
